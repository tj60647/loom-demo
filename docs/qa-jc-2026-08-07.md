2026; screenshots re-shot 11 Aug on the same dev build. Nineteen items, one line

- **Reading header — 21.** [Workbench.tsx:251](../src/components/Workbench.tsx#L251)

## Wave 1 — the contracts the app is not honouring

The four table rows above, plus the paste-tidy that item 1 shares with the
reading-text track. Each is small; together they close the largest gap between
what has been decided and what a student can do.

**1.1 · Unlabeled Passages at capture (item 4).** Save Passage stops requiring a
concept. `addPassage([])` is already legal; the modal has to stop guarding it,
and 01 Reading's Your Work panel has to show the result — an unlabeled passage
that saves and then cannot be seen is worse than one that will not save. The
graph-view unattached group is filed as P1.9 in
[contracts.md:958](contracts.md); this item and that one should land together or
the passage is invisible on 03 as well. *Decide:* what the button says when the
field is empty — "Save Passage" unchanged is the honest option, since the
concept was always optional.

**1.2 · Note/Question on a passage (item 7).** Thread `note` and `question`
through `addPassage` in
[LoomProvider.tsx:52,335](../src/components/providers/LoomProvider.tsx#L52) —
the server action already takes them — and add the third field to the capture
modal and to the Your Work edit panel. JC's diagnosis of the cost is the part to
keep: without it, *"why I grabbed this"* is typed into the concept label, and
his screenshot has a concept called **"what are propositions"** to prove it.
This item is the direct cause of at least one of the compound labels item 15
proposes to detect.

**1.3 · Rename a concept (item 5).** Wire the label into the concept panel on 04
Vocabulary, where description, merge and remove already live, and into the same
panel wherever else it opens. The action and its event kind exist. Renaming is
not merging: identity is by object, so a rename is one row and no repointing —
[naming-decisions.md](naming-decisions.md) and ruling 36 both hold unchanged.
*Guard:* two concepts may legally end up with the same label. Warn as
[OpenTab.tsx:222](../src/components/tabs/OpenTab.tsx#L222) already does at
coin-time; never refuse.

**1.4 · Make the concept typeahead visible (item 9).** The `<datalist>` at
[CaptureModal.tsx:128](../src/components/pdf/CaptureModal.tsx#L128) already
offers every concept the student owns; nothing on screen suggests it. Replace it
with a filtered list that appears under the field as you type, and show the
count of the concept's existing passages beside each row so reuse reads as
reuse. This is the same problem the word-chips below it already solved for a
different question — the chips are visible, the datalist is not.

**1.5 · Port the paste-tidy to capture (item 1, shared).** `tidy()` already
rejoins hyphenated line breaks
([clothMath.ts:91](../src/lib/clothMath.ts#L91)) and is wired into exactly one
path — pasting into the hand-capture field
([OpenTab.tsx:427](../src/components/tabs/OpenTab.tsx#L427)). Capture from the
page never calls it. **Read the reading-text plan before changing this:** the
passage text is what highlight offsets index into, and tidying the stored string
without understanding the two-strings rule
([reading-quality.md](reading-quality.md), *"Two strings, and why"*) will move
every anchor on the page. The safe shape is almost certainly tidy-on-display and
tidy-into-the-word-chips, leaving the anchored substrate alone.

## Wave 3 — the reading surface

**3.1 · Highlights as one continuous stroke (item 13).** *The largest single
piece of work on this list, and it is not cosmetic.* Highlights are painted by
mark.js over the pdf.js text layer
([PdfViewer.tsx:786–825](../src/components/pdf/PdfViewer.tsx#L786)), which emits
one `<span>` per text item — usually a word — with the space between items
carried as *positioning, not characters*. Every `<mark>` therefore gets its own
background and its own `border-bottom`
([PdfViewer.tsx:1102](../src/components/pdf/PdfViewer.tsx#L1102)), and the gaps
between them contain no element to paint. **No CSS closes a gap between separate
elements**; `box-decoration-break` fixes wrapped inline boxes, not siblings.

The fix is a highlight *layer*: take the marked `Range`, call
`getClientRects()`, merge the rects that share a line box, and draw
absolutely-positioned rectangles behind the text layer. This is how every
production PDF viewer draws a highlight. It has consequences worth planning for
rather than discovering:

- it must survive zoom, Fit Page/Fit Width, the 2-page spread, and fullscreen —
  all of which move the text layer under it;
- the click target moves with it, so `bindHighlightNode`
  ([PdfViewer.tsx:244](../src/components/pdf/PdfViewer.tsx#L244)) and the
  tooltip need re-hosting;
- `aria-label` and `tabindex` currently ride on the `<mark>` elements and must
  not be lost to a decorative layer;
- the Passages Overlay heat (`loom-overlay-heat`) nests *under* a student's own
  highlight by design, and that stacking is load-bearing — see the comment at
  [PdfViewer.tsx:747](../src/components/pdf/PdfViewer.tsx#L747).

Size it as its own piece of work. It is not a sibling of the other items in this
wave.

**3.2 · Open at the first text page (item 14).** `initialPageNumber` is a prop
with one caller ([Workbench.tsx:346](../src/components/Workbench.tsx#L346)) and
no notion of where the text starts. [pdfStructure.ts](../src/lib/pdfStructure.ts)
already distinguishes a scan, a blank leaf and a cover — it renders text-free
pages and measures ink before calling one a scan, and on one 163-page book found
eighteen blanks and a cover. So the data exists at ingest. Store a
`firstTextPage` on the source when pages are ingested, default the viewer to it,
and keep the student's last page within a session so "even mid-session it
returns to pages 1–2" stops being true. *Note:* JC reports the mid-session
regression separately from the cold open; they may be two bugs, and the
session-persistence half may be the whole of it.

**3.3 · Fewer bars (item 12).** Five bands sit between the window top and the
page: app header, reading title (`.scopebar`), search, the station bar, the
viewer toolbar. Two candidate moves, and they are independent:

- **Remove one search box.** There are genuinely two —
  [ShelfSearch.tsx:138](../src/components/shelf/ShelfSearch.tsx#L138), whose
  placeholder becomes *"search this reading…"* when a `sourceId` is present, and
  the viewer's own in-page search (`searchOpen`, with a ⌘F-style binding). They
  search the same text. *TJ's call:* which survives. The model (§Search) puts
  "this Reading" in the Reading tab and scopes 2–4 in a persistent field, which
  argues for keeping the viewer's and letting the persistent field mean
  everything-else — but that reading of §Search should be confirmed, not
  assumed.
- **Auto-hide while reading.** Collapse the header and the scope bar on scroll
  or on a fullscreen-lite toggle. Note the existing constraint: fixed chrome in
  this app has a history of eating clicks and clipping tips
  ([globals.css:378–395](../src/app/globals.css#L378), and the tips rework of
  2026-08-09). Anything that hides on scroll must not hide the control a tip is
  pointing at.

## Wave 4 — the vocabulary surfaces

**4.1 · Merge where the lists are (item 6).** `mergeConcepts` is on the provider
([LoomProvider.tsx:49](../src/components/providers/LoomProvider.tsx#L49)) and
the picker on 04 Vocabulary already resolves by **object id, not label** — a
deliberate fix, because homonyms are legal and a label cannot tell two concepts
apart ([open-work.md](open-work.md) §0.2). Reuse that picker; do not write a
second one that matches on strings. Places: the 03 Knowledge Graph sort list and
the 02 Linking left pane. A pointer to 04 is acceptable for one of them if the
control is too heavy for a sort row.

**4.2 · Coaching in both places (item 8).** The naming coaching — *"a short noun
phrase…"*, the word-chips, the "still stuck?" ladder — lives in
[CaptureModal.tsx:126–172](../src/components/pdf/CaptureModal.tsx#L126) and
nowhere else. The Your Work edit panel on 01 Reading is where a student
*renames* under the same pressure, and it offers none of it. Extract the block
so there is one copy of the words, and mount it in both. Once 1.3 lands, the
edit panel is also where renaming happens, which raises the value of this.

**4.3 · Counted mirrors over the concept list (items 15).** Two mirrors, both
counted and neither advising — near-duplicate labels (*"same idea? you decide"*)
and verb-in-label (*"threads in disguise?"*). Red line 3 is the constraint:
*counted, never judged — no scoring, no completion states, no advice*, and
[open-work.md](open-work.md) §0.4 records what happens when a mirror slips into
advice. So the mirror **names what it counted and offers the gesture**; it does
not rank, and it does not say a label is wrong.

The existing `// --- mirror counts` block in
[MapTab.tsx:282](../src/components/tabs/MapTab.tsx#L282) is a different thing —
tier and placement tallies — so this is new code, not an extension. Two useful
precedents: `STOP` and `contentWords`
([utils.ts:1](../src/lib/utils.ts#L1)) give a starting stoplist, and the
homonym confirm already decides what "the same label" means. Near-duplicate
needs a real rule (case and whitespace folding at minimum; stemming is a
decision, not a default). JC's own screenshot supplies the test set: *"concept
maps are hierarchical"*, *"arg that people 'think with concepts'"*, *"concepts
maps can help to improve thinking"*.

Note the loop back to 1.2: a Note/Question field is the *upstream* fix for
compound labels. Both are worth having; only one of them prevents the problem.

## Decisions, not work

Five things the code cannot settle. Each blocks or reshapes an item above.

1. **Item 18 — where vocabulary cleanup lives.** JC's proposal is explicit that
   it is the group's call: bring 04's editing capabilities (rename, merge,
   duplicate prompts) into the top of 02 Linking's concept list — cheaper, keeps
   04 as the cross-reading room — or actually reorder the tabs so Vocabulary
   sits between Reading and Linking. The capability move composes cleanly with
   1.3 and 4.1 and could ride with them. **The tab reorder does not**: station
   numbers are derived from the visible list
   ([JourneyNav.tsx](../src/components/ui/JourneyNav.tsx)), every piece of
   student copy names a station, and `?tab=` still speaks the legacy keys. Take
   the reorder as its own decision or not at all.
2. **Item 17 — the model doc, not the app.** §2 line 144 of
   [loom-model-build.md](loom-model-build.md) still lists *"Library · Reading ·
   Linking · Knowledge Graph · Vocabulary · Keep"*. `a731f67` dissolved Keep.
   Update the doc, or re-ratify Keep and revert the commit. The doc is the
   authority per [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md), so it cannot be left disagreeing
   with the build.
3. **Item 12 — which search box survives.** See 3.3.
4. **Item 2 — which concept count rule wins on 01 Reading.** See 2.2.
5. **Item 16 — the student-raised text flag.** Belongs to the reading-text
   track; the decision is whether a student's report reaches faculty as a queue
   entry or as a note on the reading. See
   [reading-text-track.md](reading-text-track.md).
